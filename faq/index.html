FAQ:

Common questions about custom systems, business automation and how we work together.

How much does a custom business system cost in Malaysia?

BOS Technology scopes every project around what you actually need. Pricing depends on complexity — from a single automated workflow to a full connected system. We discuss operations first, then quote. There is no fixed package that forces unused features.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on scope and how ready the requirements are. During Discover we map operations, constraints and priorities, then agree a realistic delivery path before Design and Build begins.

Do you serve businesses across Malaysia?

Yes. BOS Technology serves businesses across Malaysia. Delivery can be remote nationwide. Details on where we are based are on the Service Areas page.

What is the difference between BOS Technology and a freelance developer?

We treat each engagement as a business operations problem, not only a coding task. We start from how operations run, then design and build the system — website, software or workflow — as one connected layer, through Discover, Design and Build, and Launch and Improve.

Do I need to know technical terms to work with you?

No. Discover is about operations, goals and constraints in plain language. We translate that into system design. You do not need frameworks, APIs or architecture terms to start.

Why do businesses rely more and more on automation tools?

As businesses grow, orders, customers, people, supply chains and data become more complex. Manual work alone struggles to keep pace. Automation reduces repetitive labour, lowers error rates and helps teams process work faster.

What is the most direct benefit of using a business system?

Efficiency. Repetitive work such as data entry, approvals, reports, notifications, reconciliation and inventory updates can run through the system, so people spend time on higher-value work.

Can automation tools help a business cut costs?

Yes. Automation reduces repetitive manual effort and the losses that come from human error, missing information and costly handoffs. The larger the operation, the clearer that saving usually becomes.

How does a business system reduce human error?

Fixed workflows, permissions, data validation and automatic calculation reduce mistakes in manual steps. For example, after an order is submitted the system can check stock, calculate amounts and move the work to the next approval.

Why should a business centralise operational data in a system?

When data lives across Excel, email, chat and paper, it is hard to get a complete, accurate picture quickly. A business system holds customers, orders, inventory, finance and related records in one place so leaders can see how work is running.

Why does a business need standardised processes?

If work depends mainly on individual know-how, staff changes can hurt quality and speed. Standardising processes in a business system turns part of that experience into organisational capability, so operations are less dependent on any one person.

Why does cross-team collaboration need a business system?

End-to-end work usually spans several departments. Without one shared system, teams fall out of sync, re-enter the same data and miss handoffs. A business system lets sales, support, warehouse, purchasing and finance work from the same data and process.

How is AI different from a traditional business system?

Traditional business systems execute defined rules for deterministic work such as order handling, inventory and approvals. AI can also work with unstructured text, images and voice, and support analysis, generation and judgement. That moves systems from automatic execution toward intelligent assistance.

What is the ultimate goal of using automation tools?

The goal is not to own more software. It is to complete work at lower cost, with more stable processes and faster cycle times — while freeing people from repetitive labour.

What business problem do Business Websites and Web Platforms solve?

Many company websites look right but sit outside operations — leads and orders still live in email and chat. Business websites and web platforms connect the public site to real work: conversion paths and, when needed, CRM, orders or fulfilment — so fewer handoffs and fewer gaps.

What business problem does Custom Software for Operations solve?

Off-the-shelf products often force teams to work around the tool. Custom software is shaped to how work actually happens — including web and mobile apps — so processes fit, workarounds shrink and stacked tools stop draining time.

What business problem do Business Systems — CRM and ERP solve?

When customers, orders and inventory sit in spreadsheets and separate tools, leaders lack a clear view. CRM and ERP-style systems connect customers, orders, inventory, membership and admin backends in one layer, cutting re-entry and mismatched data.

What business problem does Workflow and Process Automation solve?

Approvals, data entry and moving information between tools consume hours. Workflow and process automation uses defined flows, integrations and practical automation so repeatable steps run in the system — with fewer delays and fewer missed steps.

What business problem does Digital Transformation and Integration solve?

Many businesses already run several systems that do not talk to each other. Digital transformation and integration maps what you have, connects data and processes, and pulls scattered tools into one workable operating layer — instead of adding another disconnected product.
