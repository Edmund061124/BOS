Business Systems — CRM and ERP

Customers, orders and inventory scattered across spreadsheets and separate tools?

The problem

When customer lists live in one place, orders in another and stock in a third, every handoff becomes a risk: double entry, missed updates and no single view of what is true right now. That friction grows as the business grows.

What we build

Business systems — CRM, ERP-style cores, order and inventory management, membership platforms and admin backends — engineered around how the business operates, not a vendor default template.

  • CRM matched to real sales and follow-up flow
  • Order and inventory views the team can trust daily
  • Membership or customer portals when needed
  • Admin backends that keep operations controllable without extra tools

Who this fits

Growing teams that have outgrown ad-hoc tools, and established businesses that need a cleaner core without buying a heavyweight package they will only half use.

Where this applies

  • Retail stock and members need one shared record across outlets
  • Leads sit in chat while jobs sit in spreadsheets
  • Order status, billing and fulfilment each live in a different tool
  • Membership renewals and entitlements need control without three extra platforms
