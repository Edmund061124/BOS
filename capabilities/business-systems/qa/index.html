Business Systems — CRM and ERP

Questions about custom CRM and ERP-style business systems — what custom means, then how delivery runs.

Understanding this custom work

What is the main difference between a custom business system and buying off-the-shelf CRM or ERP?

Ready-made products follow generic modules. A custom business system is built around your objects, fields, statuses and permissions — the system follows your structure, not the other way around.

What do businesses usually custom-build a business system to manage?

Commonly customers, orders, inventory, membership, work orders or admin backends — whatever you specify must be managed in one place. What is included follows your list, not a fixed all-in package.

When does a business need a custom business system instead of adding more spreadsheets?

When records grow, more people collaborate, and status must be tracked — and spreadsheets or chat become hard to search or reconcile. If volume is still small and one person can keep control, spreadsheets may still be enough for a while.

How are modules and rules decided in a custom business system?

From your confirmed objects and rules: what to manage, how status moves, who can view or edit, and which modules ship in phase one. You are not forced into unused modules.

After you decide to customise

What are the usual steps from mapping to delivery for a custom business system?

Typical path: map objects and rules → confirm module scope → build → confirm checkpoints → delivery. If legacy data must move in, that is scoped separately.

What usually drives how long the work takes?

Number of modules, rule complexity, data migration, integrations, and confirmation speed. Clearer scope means a clearer timeline.

How are fields, permissions and flows confirmed in custom work?

You state business requirements; they are turned into field, permission and flow notes for your confirmation. Build follows that confirmation; later changes go through change confirmation again.

After delivery, how do added modules or rule changes continue as custom work?

Describe the new module or rule change, confirm the new scope, then continue. Systems can grow in phases — phase one need not include every future idea.
