Business Websites and Web Platforms

A website that looks right but does not connect to how the business runs?

The problem

Many business websites stop at appearance. Pages look current, but enquiries, orders and inventory still live elsewhere. When the site sits apart from operations, every lead becomes another manual handoff.

What we build

Business websites and web platforms shaped around how visitors become customers and how the team works day to day.

  • Corporate and brand sites structured for real conversion paths
  • E-commerce and custom platforms that can connect into the wider stack
  • Admin-friendly publishing without a one-size template
  • Integrations where the site should talk to CRM, orders or fulfilment

Who this fits

Teams that need a public web presence that behaves like part of the business — not a brochure that goes stale when operations change.

Where this applies

  • Enquiry follow-up still lives only in email and chat
  • Product or service pages need live stock or booking status
  • A template site no longer matches how sales actually close
  • One web front door should feed the same customer record the team uses inside
