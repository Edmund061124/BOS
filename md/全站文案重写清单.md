标题
FAQ — Custom Business Systems and Digital Operations | BOS Technology

摘要
Common questions about working with BOS Technology: pricing approach, timing, Malaysia service coverage, and how we differ from freelancers or large agencies.

导航
同首页

问一
How much does a custom business system cost in Malaysia?

答一
BOS Technology scopes every project around what you actually need. Pricing depends on complexity — from a single automated workflow to a full connected system. We discuss operations first, then quote. There is no fixed package that forces unused features.

问二
How long does a project take?

答二
Timing depends on scope and how ready the requirements are. During Discover we map operations, constraints and priorities, then agree a realistic delivery path before Design and Build begins.

问三
Do you serve businesses across Malaysia?

答三
Yes. BOS Technology serves businesses across Malaysia. Delivery can be remote nationwide. Details on where we are based are on the Service Areas page.

问四
What is the difference between BOS Technology and a freelance developer?

答四
We treat each engagement as a business operations problem, not only a coding task. We start from how operations run, then design and build the system — website, software or workflow — as one connected layer, through Discover, Design and Build, and Launch and Improve.

问五
What is the difference between BOS Technology and a big agency?

答五
Large agencies often sell packages, retainers and layers of process. BOS Technology keeps the engagement scoped to what you need: custom-built around operations, priced around that scope, without a template product or a catalogue of unused features.

问六
Do I need to know technical terms to work with you?

答六
No. Discover is about operations, goals and constraints in plain language. We translate that into system design. You do not need frameworks, APIs or architecture terms to start.

问七
Why do businesses end up with disconnected tools?

答七
Needs arrive one at a time. A website vendor, a software vendor, a CRM, an automation tool — each solves a slice. Over time the stack grows, data does not sync and nobody owns the full operating picture. BOS Technology builds toward one connected operating layer instead.

问八
Can a business system reduce reliance on WhatsApp and Excel for core work?

答八
Yes, for core processes that should not depend on chat threads and spreadsheets. WhatsApp and Excel can still support communication. The system should hold customers, orders, approvals and status so work does not live only in messages and files.

问四
BOS Technology 和自由职业开发者有什么不同？

答四
我们把每次合作当作业务运营问题，而不只是写代码。我们从运营方式出发，再把网站、软件或工作流构建成一层互联体系，路径为：发现 → 设计与构建 → 上线与改进。

问五
BOS Technology 和大型代理商有什么不同？

答五
大型代理商常卖套餐、长期顾问费和层层流程。BOS Technology 把合作收束到真正需要的部分：围绕运营定制构建、按该范围报价，不强迫接受模板产品或用不到的功能目录。

合作需要懂技术术语吗？

答六
不需要。发现阶段用清楚的语言讨论运营、目标与约束。我们会把它翻译成系统设计。你不必先懂框架、API 或架构术语。

问七
为什么很多企业会堆出互不相通的工具？

答七
需求往往一次只来一块：网站、软件、CRM、自动化，各自解决一部分。时间久了，系统变多、数据不同步，也没有人掌握完整运营图景。BOS Technology 的方向是一层互联运营体系。
