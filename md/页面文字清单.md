### FAQ 1

- How much does a custom business system cost in Malaysia?
- BOS scopes every project around what you actually need, so pricing depends on complexity — from a single automated workflow to a full connected system. We discuss your operations first, then quote, with no fixed package forcing you to pay for features you do not need.

### FAQ 2

- How long does a project take?
- Timing depends on scope and how ready the requirements are. During Discover we map operations, constraints and priorities, then agree a realistic delivery path before Design & Build begins — rather than promising a fixed number of weeks up front.

### FAQ 3

- Do you only serve Kuching and Sarawak?
- BOS is based in Kuching, Sarawak, and serves businesses across Malaysia. Local clients can meet in person when it helps; businesses elsewhere in Malaysia can work with us remotely.

### FAQ 4

- What's the difference between BOS and a freelance developer?
- BOS approaches each engagement as a business operating problem, not only a coding task. We start from how your operations run, then design and build the system — website, software or workflow — as one connected layer, with a clear Discover → Design & Build → Launch & Improve path.

### FAQ 5

- What's the difference between BOS and a big agency?
- Large agencies often sell packages, retainers and layers of process. BOS keeps the engagement scoped to what you actually need: custom-built around your operations, priced around that scope, without forcing a template product or a catalogue of unused features.

### FAQ 6

- Do I need to know technical terms to work with you?
- No. Discover is about your operations, goals and constraints in plain language. We translate that into system design — you do not need to know frameworks, APIs or architecture terms to start the conversation.
